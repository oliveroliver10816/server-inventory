Two things to know before you choose
  1. Deleting projects barely helps. All 280 projects together are 24 GB of the 93 GB. The other 58 GB is cache and scratch that rebuilds itself — clearing that costs no work at all and is why the disk keeps refilling. Sort the second table by size and start there.
  2. Two folders are running right now and are marked IN USE NOW: altvaulz-x (cron, ran today) and theeveningbrief (257 live PHP processes). Separately, those 257 processes have been piling up since 21 Aug and hold 3.3 GB of RAM on a 14 GB box with no swap — that is a leak worth a look, but I have not touched it.
